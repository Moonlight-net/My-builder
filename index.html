Connect a custom domain

Use a domain you own for your published website.

Hosting required: A custom domain can only go live after this site is published on a hosting provider. Enter the DNS target supplied by that provider; My Builder does not currently publish sites, verify DNS, or issue SSL certificates.

Next steps

  1. Publish or export your site to a hosting provider that supports custom domains.
  2. At your domain registrar, add the DNS record(s) the host gives you. For a www subdomain this is commonly a CNAME, but follow your host's exact instructions.
  3. Save your domain here, then use your host's dashboard to verify DNS and enable HTTPS.
